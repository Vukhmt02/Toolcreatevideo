Imagine standing on the eastern coast of North America nearly 400 years ago. There are no skyscrapers, no highways, no massive cities stretching across the horizon. And there is no United States of America. Instead, there are vast forests, winding rivers, mountains, coastal villages, farms, trading communities, and hundreds of indigenous nations whose histories on this continent reach back thousands  of years.
Along the Atlantic coast, however, something is beginning to change.
